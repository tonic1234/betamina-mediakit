Frequently Asked Questions

What is Betamina and what is its visual identity?
Betamina is a multisport and casino platform geared toward the LATAM player. Its identity is built on an energetic concept: dynamic lines, aggressive diagonals, vibrant blues and geometric typography — all designed to convey drive, speed and fun.
What does each Mediakit pack include?

Logos → logos, favicon and official isotypes in SVG, PNG and PDF

Broadcast Assets → layouts, overlays and animations for streamers in MOGRT, PNG seq and AE

What is the official typeface?
The institutional typeface is FT Calhern, a contemporary geometric sans serif. It is recommended to use it exclusively across all official communications to maintain visual system coherence.
What are the official colors?
The main palette consists of three blues and two neutrals:

Primary Blue #0068F9 — Pantone 2727 C
Secondary Blue #0048C5 — Pantone 293 C
Tertiary Blue #0039AC — Pantone 294 C
White #FFFFFF and Light Gray #E2E2E2 — Cool Gray 1 C
Is there a clear space rule for the logo?
Yes. The logo requires a clear area defined by the X module (distance between the baseline of "BETAMINA" and the bottom edge of the container), applied to all four sides. No graphic elements should invade that zone.
Can I use these assets in client projects or my own production?
These resources are optimized for professional production — agencies, designers and developers working with the brand. All formats are vector or high resolution, ready to implement with no additional adjustments.
